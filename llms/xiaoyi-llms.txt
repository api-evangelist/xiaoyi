# Xiaoyi Technology (小蚁科技)

> Xiaoyi Technology, operated by Shanghai Yunyi Zhilian Technology (上海云蚁智联) and known for its YI-brand cameras, is a Shanghai-based smart-imaging company from the Xiaomi ecosystem. It builds action cameras, smart home/security cameras, dash cams, VR cameras, and gimbals, and offers computer vision, edge intelligence, and edge computing solutions for smart mobility, smart home, smart retail, smart parks, and smart communities.
